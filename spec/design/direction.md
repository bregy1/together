# Design direction — Yaruno

**Decided by:** the boss, with Romi as the designer. **Source:** Romi's "Yaruno Brand Guide v1.0" (November 2026, `design/brand/Yaruno_Brand_Guide_v1.0.pdf`), received 2026-11-09, merged into this file and `tokens.md`. It replaces the look of the ChatGPT prototype. Logo and colours are approved by the founding team; deviations are possible only with a reason and after talking to Romi (guide p.16). Where the guide differs from behaviour already decided in the spec, the spec applies until the boss decides (see the last section).

## Brand

- **Why Yaruno exists:** people are more connected digitally than ever, yet it is hard to do something spontaneously or meet new people. Yaruno brings people together in real life: through plans, not profiles ("über Pläne, nicht über Profile").
- **Stance:** an app you open so you can close it again quickly, because you are out. Success is measured in encounters, not in minutes on the screen. Small groups, real places, commitment: "Wer zusagt, kommt. Wer kommt, wird wieder eingeladen."
- **We are:** an activities app; local, Oberwallis first; for small groups; direct, warm and a bit cheeky; an outdoor and lifestyle brand.
- **We are not:** a dating app; a social network with likes; an event ticket shop; a club with mandatory membership; loud, glossy or over-produced.

## Brand personality

- **Direct and warm:** says what is planned, where and when; invites, never pushes.
- **A bit cheeky:** light humour where nothing is at stake; Swiss-German colour ("Hesch Zyt?").
- **Trustworthy and calm:** honest about commitment; safety is felt, not shouted.
- **Local and down-to-earth:** from locals for locals; recognisable Oberwallis places.

Never: like a dating app, a social network chasing likes, or a big corporate service.

## Name and claim

- **Name:** Yaruno, an invented word that sounds the same in German and English (ja-RU-no): three open syllables, no umlaut. In Japanese, "yaru no?" colloquially means "shall we do it?".
- **Writing the name:** in text always "Yaruno" (capital Y), never "YARUNO" or "yaruno". In the wordmark always lowercase. Never "die Yaruno-App", just "Yaruno"; "auf Yaruno", not "in Yaruno".
- **Main claim:** "Less scrolling. More living." Always on two lines, the second line in Mint. English, because it is shorter than any German version and works in bilingual Valais.
- **Further messages:** "Good plans. Better company." · "Your next adventure starts with people." · "Don't wait for someone to have time." · "Keine Dating-App. Kein Swipen. Nur echte Pläne." German messages for Instagram captions and the landing page; English ones for posters, stickers and wordmark lock-ups.

## Logo

- **The wordmark is the logo**, with no symbol beside it: "yaruno", where the last "o" becomes a filled Mint dot (the meeting point, the "I'm in", the pin on the map).
- **Construction:** Fraunces, weight 900, SOFT 100, optical size 144, tracking −2 %. Dot diameter 0.56 × font size (the x-height), gap to the "n" 0.06 × font size, sitting on the baseline; always round, always filled. Cooper Black is a licensed alternative (commercial; clarify before use).
- **Variants:** Primary (Eis and Mint on Nacht) for the app, website, Instagram profile and posters. On Mint for stickers, the app-icon variant and highlights. On light for documents, daylight screens and partner logos on white. Single colour for stamps and engraving, with the dot in the same colour. Never Mint on light (1.1:1, invisible).
- **Clear space and minimum size:** clear space equals the dot's diameter on every side; nothing inside it. Minimum width 90 px on screen, 24 mm in print; below that only the short mark.
- **Short mark:** "y" with the dot, for the favicon, avatars and loading indicators. Never the dot alone, because it is generic.
- **App icon:** standard is Nacht with an Eis "y" and the Mint dot; the Mint variant for announcements and stickers. Export 1024 × 1024 px without rounding (the stores round); the short mark fills 60 % of the edge.
- **Don'ts:** no distorting, stretching or rotating; no colours other than Eis, Nacht and Mint; no gradient, shadow or outline; never replace the dot with an icon, emoji or image; never in capitals, in Archivo or another font (the serif belongs to the wordmark only); never directly on a photo without a Nacht or Eis area beneath; no suffixes ("Yaruno Wallis", "Yaruno Pro").

## Colour: palette "Gletscher"

Values and contrast checks are in `tokens.md`.

- **Nacht is the standard look**: deep green-black, Gletscherblau for surfaces, Eis-Mint as the only accent, for an app people open in the evening and photos that glow on dark. A daylight variant uses the same colours with reversed roles, for screens in the sun and for documents.
- **60 / 30 / 10:** one ground colour (Nacht), one text colour (Eis), one accent (Mint). There are no more than three real colours; everything else is greyscale or photo. Lines and dividers in Linie.
- **The five rules:**
  1. **Mint is a surface:** buttons, tags, markers and the dot, always with Nacht text on it. Never two Mint elements side by side; one Mint button per screen.
  2. **Exception on dark:** on Nacht, Mint may also be text, for headlines from 20 px and single words in the claim.
  3. **Never Mint on light:** on a light background the accent becomes Nacht or Gletscherblau.
  4. **Secondary text in Nebel, never lighter;** on light, Nebel becomes #56635F.
  5. **Status never by colour alone:** about 8 % of men tell red and green apart poorly, so there is always an icon or a word.
- **New combinations** are contrast-checked before use; 4.3:1 has failed, not "almost passed".

## Typography

- **Archivo for everything** (one family, width axis 62–125 %, so narrow tags and wide poster lines come from the same file). **Fraunces Black Soft only in the wordmark and the short mark**, never in headlines, buttons or text, so the logo stays the only serif and stands out.
- **Headlines** in Archivo 700, no capitals, tracking −3 %, line height 1.02: Swiss International Style, quiet, neutral and trustworthy. The photos carry the emotion; the type holds back.
- **Weights:** 400 body text, 500 chips and meta, 600 emphasis and labels, 700 buttons and numbers. Labels in capitals with tracking +8 %.
- **Rules:** one family, no second or substitute font, also not on Instagram. Capitals only for labels and tags. Body text never under 14 px, lines at most 70 characters. Numbers (05:30, 3 von 6) in 700 with tabular figures. Italics only in quotes; no underlines except text links. Print: headlines from 14 pt, text from 9 pt.

## Imagery

Photos look as if someone from the group took them, not an agency: point-and-shoot, evening light, the Alps in the background, people in the foreground doing something.

- **Yes:** real participants with their consent, no models; moments during the activity, not poses before it; morning and evening light, fog, snow, wet streets; recognisable Oberwallis places (valley stations, train stations, squares, halls); slight blur, grain and flash at night are fine; darken slightly only where text sits on a photo; no filters, no Mint tint.
- **No:** stock photos of laughing groups and high-fives; Matterhorn postcards or drone panoramas without people; perfect gear staging or brand logos up front; couples looking at each other (Yaruno is not a dating app); chat screenshots; faces of minors; AI-generated people.
- **Placeholders in the app:** an area in Gletscherblau, Moos or Schiefer-Blau with one line of text, never a grey image icon.

## UI building blocks

- **Buttons:** primary in Mint with Nacht text, Archivo 700, 60 px high, fully rounded; secondary as an Eis outline; tertiary as underlined text. One primary button per screen; touch areas at least 44 × 44 px.
- **Chips and tags:** chips filter (active = filled with Eis); tags describe (category in Mint capitals, time in Nacht). Never more than one Mint tag per card.
- **Inputs and switches:** fields in Schiefer with a Linie border, radius 16 px, 56 px high. Switch track in Eis, knob in Mint when on (see the contrast findings in `tokens.md`).
- **Cards:** radius 24 px, image area at the top (150 px), title in Archivo 600 / 20 px, meta in Archivo 400 / 13 px in Nebel. Cards lie on Nacht, never on Schiefer.
- **Layout:** 8 px grid with spacing 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 20 · 24 · 32; side margin 20 px.
- **Icons:** 2 px stroke, round ends, 20–24 px; never emoji.
- **Platforms:** one shared brand look on iOS and Android; platform conventions only where users expect them (back gesture, share sheet, system permission dialogs).
- **Onboarding:** one step per screen with a segmented progress bar ("Schritt 1 von 5"), as in the ChatGPT prototype.

## Reference screens

Romi's guide (p.12) shows three core screens from her click prototype v1 (eight screens, kept in her design canvas): Entdecken, a plan, and Profil. What we take for the look:

- **Entdecken:** small wordmark top left, a region chip at the top right, a two-line headline ("Was geht diese Woche?"), filter chips ("Für dich", "Heute", "Outdoor"), plan cards with a Mint category tag, a time tag, the title and one line of meta ("3 von 6 dabei"), one primary button ("Plan erstellen"), a bottom tab bar.
- **Plan:** image area with tags, title, host line, an info card with the plan's details, a short note on commitment ("Wenn du zusagst, rechnen die anderen mit dir."), one primary button.
- **Profil:** avatar, first name, a card with the trust label, small counters.

Image areas stay placeholders until real photos from the pilot exist. Several details in these mock-ups differ from decided behaviour; see the last section.

## Tone of voice

Like a colleague texting you: "Hesch Zyt? Wir gehen um sechs." Short, direct, warm, no marketing tone. We ask, we invite, we don't push.

- **German (source):** *du*, always, also in summaries of the terms of use. Verbs before nouns ("Plan erstellen", not "Aktivitätserstellung"). Concrete: place, time, number ("Sa 05:30, Talstation Blatten, 3 von 6"). Honest about commitment: we say that others count on you. Swiss spelling: "ss" instead of "ß"; "Velo", "Kafi", "Znüni" are welcome. English only in the claim; the German UI speaks German.
- **French:** [NEEDS DECISION: French form of address — options: A) tu, B) vous — recommended: A, because it matches the German du and the local, informal personality]
- **English:** informal, matching the German tone.
- **Microcopy** (guide p.13; the glossary still decides the terms):

| Moment | Say | Don't say |
|---|---|---|
| Leaving a plan | "Doch nicht" | "Teilnahme stornieren" |
| Creating | "Plan erstellen" | "Neue Aktivität anlegen" |
| Empty feed | "Nichts dabei? Mach den ersten Schritt." | "Keine Ergebnisse gefunden." |
| Error | "Das hat nicht geklappt. Nochmal?" | "Ein unerwarteter Fehler ist aufgetreten." |
| Reminder | "Morgen 05:30, Lea rechnet mit dir." | "Erinnerung: Bevorstehende Aktivität." |

| Joining | "Bin dabei" (sends a request the host accepts) | "Teilnehmen" |

The guide's lines for the deposit and rating are not used (see the last section).

## Outside the app

- **Instagram:** square tile with the wordmark top left, a message in Archivo 700 with the last line in Mint and a hint at the bottom, or a photo with a small wordmark and no text. Story (9:16): photo, three short lines, wordmark at the bottom.
- **Stickers and merch:** a round Mint sticker with the short mark; a pill-shaped Nacht sticker with the wordmark.
- **Landing page:** Nacht background, wordmark, main claim, one sentence, an e-mail field, optionally region and interests, a privacy notice, and three cards like in the app; its only job is to measure demand. It collects personal data, so it needs the privacy policy (`app.md` → Legal).
- **Store graphics:** `app.md` → Stores.

## Accessibility

Level: see `constitution.md`. The guide's rules support it: every text pair contrast-checked, status never by colour alone, touch areas at least 44 × 44 px, body text at least 14 px. Two component states fail the non-text contrast criterion; they are listed in `tokens.md` for Romi. Text scales with the system text size, motion respects the system's reduced-motion setting, and every control and photo has a screen-reader label.

## Clashes between the brand guide and the spec

The spec applies until the boss decides each point.

1. **Deposit and revenue — decided: the spec applies** (boss, 2026-11-09). Guide p.12–15: a CHF 5–10 deposit against no-shows ("Bin dabei · Depot CHF 5"), later a deposit per meeting plus partnerships as the revenue model. Decision: no payments of any kind; Yaruno stays free, with voluntary support only. Reasons (boss): "I will definitely go without money"; handling money adds complexity and makes things harder. To change in the guide: the deposit on p.12, p.13 (microcopy), p.14 (principle and revenue model) and p.15 (screenshot "Depot statt Ghosting.").
2. **ID check and trust levels for hosts — decided: the spec applies** (boss, 2026-11-09). Guide p.14: an ID check for everyone who creates plans; three trust levels (Neu, Verlässlich, Stammgast) whose rights grow with meetings. Decision: anyone can host; phone verification for all; no ID check and no rights by level. Reasons (boss): an ID check is a big hurdle at the start; it may be added later (`features.md` → Ideas parking lot).
3. **How the trust label looks — decided: the spec applies** (boss, 2026-11-09). Guide p.12: "Vertrauensstufe · Verlässlich 100 %", "Noch 5 Treffen bis Stammgast", counters, and a "Stammgast" badge from ten meetings (p.15). Decision: a subtle, friendly label in words only; no number, no progress, nothing to collect. Reasons (boss, 2026-11-08): the score must not be a point system that people work for; normal members who come to what they agreed to and behave decently get an ok-to-good label without effort; it mainly flags repeated bad behaviour.
4. **Mutual rating — decided: the spec applies** (boss, 2026-11-09). Guide p.14: everyone rates everyone, blind, within 48 hours ("War da? Wieder treffen? Alles okay?"). Decision: after a plan, only who was there is confirmed. Reasons (boss, 2026-11-08): ratings must stay "really simple and small"; lateness and similar problems go through a report instead.
5. **Immediate bans — decided: the spec applies** (boss, 2026-11-09). Guide p.14: dating intentions, trading or harassment lead to an immediate ban. Decision: moderators review a report first, then decide; the member can object (`features.md` → ADM). Reason (boss, 2026-11-08): reports affect people only once they have been reviewed.
6. **Free cancellation window — decided: the guide applies** (boss, 2026-11-09). Guide p.12 and 14: cancelling up to 24 hours before is fine. Decision: a member's cancellation counts as short-term only within 24 hours of the start (was 48 hours); a host's stays at 14 days (`features.md` → TRUST).
7. **Joining wording — decided: the guide's wording, with the spec's behaviour** (boss, 2026-11-09). Guide p.11 and 13: "Bin dabei". Decision: the button reads "Bin dabei" and sends a join request; the host still accepts or declines, and the plan shows the request as "angefragt" until then (`glossary.md` → request to join; `features.md` → JOIN).
8. **Location details before acceptance.** Guide p.12: the plan screen shows the exact meeting point ("Talstation Blatten-Belalp, beim Kiosk") before joining, and cards show exact places. Spec: before acceptance only the coarse region is shown, and the server never sends the location or meeting point (`features.md` → PLAN, FEED; `vision.md` → Principles). This is not a choice: the mock-ups need to follow the spec.
9. **Profile details — decided: the spec applies** (boss, 2026-11-09). Guide p.12: the profile shows the home municipality and "dabei seit Okt 2026". Decision: neither is shown; the home region is used for the feed only (PROF-001), and there is no "member since" date.
10. **"Kein Flirten" rule — decided: reworded** (boss, 2026-11-09). Guide p.13: "Kein Flirten." Decision: the first Spielregel reads "Keine Dating-App."; consensual flirting is not forbidden, Yaruno just isn't a dating platform (`vision.md` → Non-goals). The other five rules stay as in the guide (`features.md` → MOD).
11. **Dark as the only standard — decided: the spec applies** (boss, 2026-11-09). Guide p.7: "Nacht als Standard, nicht als Option". Decision: Yaruno follows the phone's light or dark setting, with the Nacht look and the daylight look (`tokens.md`). Reason (boss, 2026-11-08): it respects members' settings, and dark screens are hard to read in the sun.
12. **Feed layout — decided: the guide applies** (boss, 2026-11-09). Decision: the feed is a list of cards with an image area on top, not Reels-like full-height cards (`features.md` → FEED).
13. **Tab bar — decided: three tabs** (boss, 2026-11-09). Decision: "Entdecken · Meine Pläne · Profil"; friends are embedded in "Meine Pläne" instead of a fourth tab (`features.md` → FRND).
14. **Reminders — decided: added** (boss, 2026-11-09). Guide p.13 shows a reminder ("Morgen 05:30, Lea rechnet mit dir."). Decision: participants get a reminder the day before a plan, on by default; like every kind of notification it can be switched off in the settings (`features.md` → NOTIF, SET).
