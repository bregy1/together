# Glossary — TOGETHER

One canonical term per concept, in every UI language. The spec, code identifiers and UI text use these terms; the words under "Avoid" are not used anywhere. Translations in *italics* are suggestions until the boss or a native speaker confirms them. French terms are confirmed by a native French speaker before French ships (boss, 2026-11-08).

| Concept | Meaning | de | fr | en | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| activity | The kind of thing done in a plan (e.g. bouldering, hiking); used as the plan's category | Aktivität | *activité* | activity | Aktivität as a name for the plan itself |
| admin | A person on the TOGETHER team who runs the app: everything a moderator does, plus cancellation reviews and the interest list | Admin | *admin* | admin | — |
| moderator | A person with the role that allows reviewing reports and deciding on them | Moderator/-in | *modérateur·rice* | moderator | — |
| block | Stop another member from interacting with you | blockieren | *bloquer* | block | — |
| cancel | Withdraw from a plan (participant) or call off a plan (host); always with a reason | absagen; die Absage | *annuler; l'annulation* | cancel; cancellation | abmelden (means "log out") |
| co-host | A member the host adds to a plan, who can edit it with the host | Co-Gastgeber | *co-hôte* | co-host | Mitbearbeiter |
| community rules | The rules every member accepts at sign-up and can read at any time | Spielregeln | *règles du jeu* | community rules | Community-Regeln |
| discover | The feed of open plans in the member's region | Entdecken | *Découvrir* | Discover | Feed (in UI text) |
| free seat | An open place in a plan | freier Platz | *place libre* | free seat | Slot |
| host | The member who created a plan and decides who joins | Gastgeber | *hôte* | host | Ersteller, Organisator, plan maker |
| interest | One of up to five topics from the curated list on a profile | Interesse | *centre d'intérêt* | interest | Tag, Hobby |
| location | Where the activity of a plan happens; required; visible only to participants | Ort | *lieu* | location | event location, Standort |
| meeting point | Optional place where the group meets before the location; visible only to participants | Treffpunkt | *point de rencontre* | meeting point | — |
| member | A person with a TOGETHER account | Mitglied | *membre* | member | User, Nutzer |
| my plans | The member's hosted, requested and accepted plans, as a list or a calendar | Meine Pläne | *Mes plans* | My plans | — |
| participant | A member who is accepted into a plan (or was invited by the host) | Teilnehmer/-in | *participant·e* | participant | — |
| partner organisation | An organisation (bar, gym, band) that publishes open events | Partner | *partenaire* | partner | Sponsor |
| plan | Something a host creates that others can join: an activity at a time and a place | Plan | *plan* | plan | Aktivität, Event, Slot, Treffen (for this concept) |
| plan chat | The chat of a plan's host and participants, shown as "Chat" inside the plan | Chat | *chat* | Chat | Gruppenchat (reserved for the later group chat) |
| region | The coarse area shown publicly, e.g. "Brig" | Region | *région* | region | Standort |
| report | Flag a member, a plan or a message to the admins | melden; die Meldung | *signaler; le signalement* | report | — |
| request to join | Ask the host to be let into a plan | Teilnahme anfragen; die Anfrage | *demander à participer; la demande* | request to join; request | Bin dabei |
| request states | requested · accepted · declined · expired · group full | angefragt · angenommen · abgelehnt · abgelaufen · Gruppe voll | *demandé · accepté · refusé · expiré · complet* | requested · accepted · declined · expired · group full | — |
| take over | A participant becomes the host when the host cancels | übernehmen | *reprendre* | take over | — |
| trust score | Internal measure of reliability; members only see a friendly label, never a number | Verlässlichkeit | *fiabilité* | reliability | Score, Punkte, Vertrauen |
| verified | A member who passed the selfie check against their photos | verifiziert | *vérifié·e* | verified | — |
