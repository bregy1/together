# Glossary — Yaruno

One canonical term per concept, in every UI language. The spec, code identifiers and UI text use these terms; the words under "Avoid" are not used anywhere. Translations in *italics* are suggestions until the boss or a native speaker confirms them. French terms are confirmed by a native French speaker before French ships (boss, 2026-11-08).

| Concept | Meaning | de | fr | en | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| activity | What a plan is about, from the curated list: either a general activity (e.g. Outdoor), which is also an interest, or a specific activity under one (e.g. Wandern under Outdoor) | Aktivität | *activité* | activity | Aktivität as a name for the plan itself, Kategorie |
| admin | A person on the Yaruno team who runs the app: everything a moderator does, plus cancellation reviews and the interest list | Admin | *admin* | admin | — |
| block | Stop another member from interacting with you | blockieren | *bloquer* | block | — |
| cancel | Withdraw from a plan (participant) or call off a plan (host); always with a reason | absagen; die Absage | *annuler; l'annulation* | cancel; cancellation | abmelden (means "log out") |
| co-host | A member the host adds to a plan, who can edit it with the host | Co-Gastgeber | *co-hôte* | co-host | Mitbearbeiter |
| community rules | The rules every member accepts at sign-up and can read at any time | Spielregeln | *règles du jeu* | community rules | Community-Regeln |
| discover | The feed of open plans in the member's region | Entdecken | *Découvrir* | Discover | Feed (in UI text) |
| former member | How a deleted account appears where it is still shown, e.g. as the author of chat messages | Ehemaliges Mitglied | *ancien membre* | former member | Gelöschter Nutzer |
| free seat | An open place in a plan | freier Platz | *place libre* | free seat | Slot |
| friend | A member with whom a friend request was accepted; friends see where the other is in and can be invited into plans | Freund, Freundin; die Freunde | *ami·e* | friend | Kontakt, Follower |
| friend request | Ask another member, by name + ID or after a shared plan, to become friends; they accept or decline | Freundschaftsanfrage | *demande d'ami* | friend request | Anfrage (means a request to join) |
| host | The member who created a plan and decides who joins | Gastgeber | *hôte* | host | Ersteller, Organisator, plan maker |
| interest | One of up to five general activities a member likes, shown on the profile (e.g. Outdoor, Sport) | Interesse | *centre d'intérêt* | interest | Tag, Hobby |
| invitation | The host asks a friend into their plan; accepting makes the friend a participant if a seat is free | Einladung | *invitation* | invitation | — |
| kept plan | A member's saved copy of a past plan, to reuse later | gemerkter Plan; merken | *plan gardé; garder* | saved plan; save | Vorlage, Favorit |
| location | Where the activity of a plan happens; required; visible only to participants | Ort | *lieu* | location | event location, Standort |
| login code | The one-time code sent by SMS to verify a phone number | Code; SMS-Code | *code; code SMS* | code | Passwort, PIN, TAN |
| meeting point | Optional place where the group meets before the location; visible only to participants | Treffpunkt | *point de rencontre* | meeting point | — |
| member | A person with a Yaruno account | Mitglied | *membre* | member | User, Nutzer |
| moderator | A person with the role that allows reviewing reports and deciding on them | Moderator/-in | *modérateur·rice* | moderator | — |
| my plans | The member's hosted, requested and accepted plans, as a list or a calendar | Meine Pläne | *Mes plans* | My plans | — |
| name + ID | A member's first name plus a four-digit number the app assigns (e.g. Lea#4821), used to add them as a friend | Name + ID | *nom + ID* | name + ID | Username, Benutzername |
| objection | A member's request to review a sanction or a no-show again, within 14 days, once | widersprechen; der Widerspruch | *contester; la contestation* | object; objection | Einsprache, Beschwerde |
| participant | A member who is accepted into a plan (or was invited by the host) | Teilnehmer/-in | *participant·e* | participant | — |
| partner organisation | An organisation (bar, gym, band) that publishes open events | Partner | *partenaire* | partner | Sponsor |
| plan | Something a host creates that others can join: an activity at a time and a place | Plan | *plan* | plan | Aktivität, Event, Slot, Treffen (for this concept) |
| plan chat | The chat of a plan's host and participants, shown as "Chat" inside the plan | Chat | *chat* | Chat | Gruppenchat (reserved for the later group chat) |
| privacy policy | The text that explains which personal data Yaruno processes and why | Datenschutzerklärung | *politique de confidentialité* | privacy policy | Datenschutzbestimmungen |
| profile | What other members can see about a member: photos, first name, age, interests, "Über mich", trust label | Profil | *profil* | profile | — |
| profile description | The optional free text on a profile, at most 300 characters | Über mich | *à propos de moi* | *About me* | Beschreibung, Bio |
| region | The coarse area shown publicly, e.g. "Brig" | Region | *région* | region | Standort |
| reopen | Give a past or cancelled plan a new date; the same plan, group and chat continue, and earlier participants confirm whether they are in again | Nochmals machen | *reprogrammer* | reopen | wiederholen, neu ansetzen |
| report | Flag a member, a plan or a message to the admins | melden; die Meldung | *signaler; le signalement* | report | — |
| request states | requested · accepted · declined · withdrawn · group full · ended at the start | angefragt · angenommen · nicht angenommen · zurückgezogen · Gruppe voll · beendet | *demandé · accepté · refusé · retiré · complet · terminé* | requested · accepted · declined · withdrawn · group full · ended | abgelaufen, abgelehnt (in UI text) |
| request to join | Ask the host to be let into a plan; the button reads "Bin dabei" and sends a request the host still has to accept | Bin dabei (button); die Anfrage | *Je suis partant·e (bouton); la demande* | *I'm in* (button); request | Teilnehmen, Beitreten, Teilnahme anfragen |
| safety tips | Short advice for meeting people from the app, shown once at the first plan and reachable at any time | Sicherheitstipps | *conseils de sécurité* | safety tips | — |
| sanction | What a moderator decides on a confirmed report: warning, hidden content, suspension for 7, 30 or 90 days, or ban | Massnahme: Verwarnung, Inhalt entfernt, Sperre (7, 30 oder 90 Tage), dauerhafte Sperre | *sanction* | sanction | Strafe |
| sign in | Get into an existing account on a device with phone number and code | anmelden; die Anmeldung | *se connecter* | sign in | Login, einloggen |
| sign up | Create a new account: phone number, code and the profile steps | Konto anlegen; die Registrierung | *créer un compte* | sign up | Account erstellen |
| take over | A participant becomes the host when the host cancels | übernehmen | *reprendre* | take over | — |
| terms of use | The legal terms every member accepts at sign-up | Nutzungsbedingungen | *conditions d'utilisation* | terms of use | AGB, EULA (in UI text) |
| trust score | Internal measure of reliability; members see a coloured bar with four fixed states (new, normal, lowered, low), never a number | Verlässlichkeit | *fiabilité* | reliability | Score, Punkte, Vertrauen |
| verified | A member who passed the selfie check against their photos | verifiziert | *vérifié·e* | verified | — |
| Yaruno | The name of the app; written "Yaruno" in text, lowercase only in the wordmark | Yaruno | Yaruno | Yaruno | TOGETHER (old working name), YARUNO, "die Yaruno-App", "in Yaruno" (say "auf Yaruno") |
