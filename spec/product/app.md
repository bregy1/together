## Permissions

| Permission | Needed for | Asked when | Without it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Notifications | Join requests, decisions, plan changes, invitations [NOTIF] | After the member's first join request, with "Nicht jetzt" [ONB] | The member sees updates only when opening the app |
| Photos / camera | Profile photos [ONB, PROF] | When the member adds a photo | No photo can be added; sign-up needs one portrait |

Not requested: location and contacts (`vision.md` → Non-goals).

## Stores

- **Store regions:** Switzerland only at launch (`vision.md` → Target users).
- **Category:** Lifestyle; not Social Networking, not Dating (brand guide p.15).
- **Age rating:** 18+; confirmed through both stores' questionnaires (ADR-0002).
- **Store graphics** (brand guide p.15): app icon 1024 × 1024 px in the Nacht variant, without rounding; screenshots on Nacht, each with one line in Archivo 700 at the top (e.g. "Pläne statt Profile.", "Max. 6 Personen."); Play Store feature graphic 1024 × 500 px with the wordmark on the left and the claim on the right.
- **Pricing:** free, with no in-app purchases (`vision.md` → Non-goals).
- **Listing languages:** the languages that ship in the first release (`constitution.md`).
- **Developer accounts:** depend on the legal entity below; an organisation account needs a D-U-N-S number.
